## Gate 2 — Channel

- **Primary channel: open solicitations and prequalified vendor pools.** RFIs,
  RFPs, co-op contract vehicles, and on-call lists — i.e. buyers who have
  already written down that they intend to spend money on this, with a date.
- **Unfair advantage that makes it work:** (a) a dated legal obligation exists
  that the buyer did not invent and cannot ignore — the DOJ Title II rule, now
  April 26 2027 / April 26 2028 after the interim final rule of April 20 2026;
  (b) 111 completed before/after veraPDF conformance reports on real published
  government documents, which is the exact artifact these solicitations ask for
  and which almost no one-person shop can produce; (c) already a registered
  Pennsylvania Commonwealth vendor (#0000571931).
- **Non-cold-email channel (required):** solicitations and vendor-pool
  registrations, plus inbound marketplace buyers (Fiverr), plus white-label
  subcontracting to firms that already hold the contracts.
- **If cold email: 100-send test result.** 107 pitches sent, **1 warm reply =
  0.93%**, below the 3% bar. **Cold email is therefore demoted and may not be
  the primary play in this lane.** It stays only as a low-cost secondary touch
  on threads that already exist.

### Channel reality check, 2026-09-09 (measured, not assumed)

The primary channel above was named before it was measured. It has now been measured, and it is thinner than assumed:

- **Open solicitations: one, and it is already answered.** All 182 currently-open PA state solicitations read first-hand; nationally only Mecklenburg qualifies, submitted 9/8. Six comparable RFPs (WA, Utah USBE, CT, NJ DOE, Fox Valley, Kansas) all posted Dec 2025 - Feb 2026 and closed. The next wave is a fiscal-year cluster, likely late 2026 / early 2027.
- **Vendor pools: one exists.** Nine states swept; only Pennsylvania has an accessibility-specific vehicle (the ITQ Digital Accessibility category). All seven cooperatives are resale networks that cannot be joined directly.

**So the channel narrows to one bet: the PA ITQ.** Rolling, home state, already a registered supplier, and it carries COSTARS to every PA local entity. The gate is a two-year experience requirement against a three-month-old LLC; the question is with DGS as of 9/9. This does not invalidate Gate 2 - cold email stays demoted at 0.93% - but it means the honest description of the channel is "one rolling vehicle plus a re-sweep every 2-3 weeks for the next RFP wave," not "a stream of open solicitations."

## Gate 3 — Kill bar

- **Kill date:** 2026-09-30
- **Kill bar:** at least **1 paid engagement**, or **3 human replies sourced
  from the non-cold channel** (a solicitation, a vendor pool, or an inbound
  marketplace buyer). Sends, leads, prospects and threads do not count toward
  this number.
- **Cancel on failure:** stop all outbound in this lane, stop building
  remediation tooling, and reallocate to the clinician-facing work where Kaleb
  is an insider rather than a stranger.

## Verdict

**Passed**, with cold email explicitly demoted. The gate that actually binds
here is Gate 2, not Gate 1: demand is documented and funded, but it was being
chased through the one channel that has never worked. The corrective action is
to sell into published solicitations and vendor pools, and to make the
already-sent outreach land on a site that describes this business instead of a
dead one.
